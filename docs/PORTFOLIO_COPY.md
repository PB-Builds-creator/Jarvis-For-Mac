## Interview explanation

> I started with a small local voice bridge and evolved it from real failures on my daily-use Mac. The central design decision was that models can propose actions but cannot authorize them. Deterministic policy grounds every proposal against the user's request, classifies risk, previews the exact plan, executes steps transactionally, and verifies outcomes. I also optimized the always-on path so the HUD and wake listener stay lightweight while larger local models load only when useful. I used AI coding tools heavily, but I owned the requirements, architecture constraints, test strategy, hardware validation, and acceptance decisions.
